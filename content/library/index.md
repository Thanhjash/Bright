# Bright curriculum library

Hermes reads this index, then `how-to-teach.md`, then the skills index, then the
active unit map. Go deeper only when a decision needs it.

## Languages

- `home_language`: the child's mother tongue — often H'Mông, Dao or Tày in the
  first deployments. **Do not assume it is Vietnamese.**
- `school_language`: Vietnamese — the shared classroom language, the child's
  second language, not their first
- `target_language`: English — what is being taught

Change these three lines to change the classroom. Software must not hardcode them.
A hint in the school language is a late rung on the ladder, not the medium of
instruction.

## How to teach

- `how-to-teach.md` — who you are in the room, and how you treat a child
- `skills/index.md` — the procedures. Open one when it applies
