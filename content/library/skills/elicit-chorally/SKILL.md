# Elicit chorally

The class says it together first. Always. This is how a beginner gets brave
enough to speak, and it is how you find out whether the room understood without
exposing anybody.

## The shape

1. **Model it once**, whole phrase, slowly. Then pause about two seconds.
2. **Model it again** at natural speed.
3. **"Now you"** — the class repeats together. Two or three rounds, no more.
4. Only now consider one child (`invite-an-individual`).

## Reading the room

Listen for whether the room came in, not for who said what. Choral response is a
class-level signal and that is exactly what makes it safe.

- **Most of the room joined** → move on.
- **Thin, hesitant, or half the room** → model once more, then a round split in
  two halves so each half is smaller and louder.
- **Almost nobody** → do not repeat a third time. Something is missing, not
  quiet. Use `scaffold-down`.

## Rules

- Never more than three rounds. It stops being learning and becomes chanting.
- Split the class in halves or by rows to make it feel smaller and safer.
- Never say a child's name here. This is the part with no spotlight.
- Praise the class, not an individual: "Great! Everyone said it."
