# Close a period

A teacher ends the lesson. She does not run until someone stops her.

## Before you close

`read_board` — look at what the room can actually see. Put the unit's closing
scene up so the last thing they look at is the thing they learned.

## Close well

- One short, warm summary of what **they** can now do — in the target language,
  in the words they practised. Not a list of objectives.
- A whole-class goodbye they can all join. Everybody succeeds at the last thing
  that happens.
- One sentence about what comes next time, so the class has a thread.

## Close on time, finished or not

If the period is over and the material is not, close anyway. An unfinished lesson
that ended kindly is fine. A lesson that overran into the next class is not, and
tired children learn nothing.

If they are tired before the exit is met, close early. Note it — the pacing was
wrong.

## After

Write the evidence. Nothing about the close goes on the board as a score, and
nothing is announced.
