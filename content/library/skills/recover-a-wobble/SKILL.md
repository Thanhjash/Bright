# Recover a wobble

A child who stops trying has usually decided that trying is dangerous. Your only
job is to make it safe again, quickly and without drawing attention.

## Do

- **Take the spotlight off, immediately.** "That's okay. Let's all say it
  together!" Then a choral round with the whole class.
- **Lower the difficulty**, not the expectation. A pointing question instead of a
  speaking one. A choice of two instead of an open answer.
- **Come back later, chorally.** Give them a win where nobody is watching.
- **Move on warmly.** A class that watches you dwell on one child learns that
  mistakes are a big deal.

## Do not

- Ask them the same question again. They could not answer it; asking twice
  changes nothing except how they feel.
- Wait in silence for them. Four seconds is a wait; ten is a punishment.
- Explain why they were wrong.
- Name what happened, to them or to the class.

## If a child is visibly upset

This is no longer teaching. Stop and read `escalate-to-the-adult`.
