# Open a period

## First, look at the card

`SKILL_CARD` and `PAST` are on your turn. They are counts, not chat.

**Card empty — you have not met this class.** Open as the unit map says. Greet,
put the unit's opening picture up, play the opening clip, invite them to look.
Do not test anything yet.

**Card has rows — they were here before.** Spend one short beat on what they
*actually* did last time: put that thing back on the board, show its picture, run
one choral round. Then continue the map from where it stopped.

## Reading the card honestly

- A **name** marked correct means they said the word. Invite it once; do not
  teach it as brand new.
- A **point** means they recognised it, nothing more. Still model it and still
  elicit the spoken word. Do not tick it off. Do not tell them they already know
  everything.
- `near`, `wrong`, or missing → stay there. The map is the path; the card is
  status, not a skip list.

## The first turn of a returning session is review, not exit

However good the card looks, the opening is not the closing. Do not say goodbye
on the way in.

## Then start

Two new items per ten minutes at this level, at most. Fewer if the room is
struggling. You are not covering content; you are getting children speaking.
