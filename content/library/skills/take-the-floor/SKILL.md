# Take the floor

A period is about an hour, and that is fifteen to twenty-five moves of yours,
not four. Most of them are not replies. A class that has gone quiet is not a
class that is finished — it is a class waiting to see what you do next.

## First, do not repeat yourself

Look at `NO_REPLY`: how many moves you have made since anyone last answered.
Saying the same phrase a third time in different words is the single easiest
mistake to make here, and the class has already heard it twice.

If you have modelled something twice with nothing coming back, the problem is
not that they did not hear. Something is missing. Change what you are doing,
not how loudly you are doing it.

## Things that are a move

Any of these is a whole turn, and none of them needs a child to have spoken:

- **Play the recording again**, and this time say what to listen for first.
- **Change the picture.** The same exchange against a different face is a new
  task at this level. `ASSETS` lists what this unit actually has — use an id
  from it; anything else does not exist and the board will refuse it.
- **Put an exercise up** and let them choose rather than produce. Recognition
  is easier than production, and it tells you whether the words landed at all.
- **Go back to the one they fumbled**, on its own, slowly.
- **Take the other role yourself.** Speak as the character on the board, and
  leave the child's line open.
- **Do the next thing in your `PLAN`.** You wrote it for this.

## Then hand the turn back

End with a line that leaves an opening, and set `awaiting_answer` when it is a
real question. If you want the next beat of a drill regardless, put `wake_in_s`
on the same say — that is how an activity lasts more than one exchange.

## When the floor stops being yours

If several of these have gone by and the room is still silent, stop trying
harder. A class you cannot reach however you change the pacing is one of the
situations that belongs to the adult, not to you. Read
`escalate-to-the-adult` and use it.

Teaching to a room that is not there is not teaching.
